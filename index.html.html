Products & Technical Specifications

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Blocks

AAC is a lightweight green building material produced from Flyash, Cement, Water, Lime, and Aluminium Powder. The microscopic air cavities contained in the block make it up to 80% air volume, offering lightweight properties with uncompromised load-bearing strength.

Key Architectural & Technical Advantages

  • Earthquake & Wind Resistant: Low structural mass reduces applied seismic load forces. Proven to withstand wind loads of Category 5 tropical storms.
  • Easy Workability: Can be easily sawn, cut, slotted, drilled, chased, and nailed using ordinary woodworking tools for quick utility installation.
  • High Water Resistance: Cellular internal structure effectively resists water penetration.

FORTEXA High-Strength Block Jointing Mortar

A versatile dry-mix mortar formulation consisting of high-grade cement, graded sand, and blended polymers designed to deliver high tensile bond strength and superior water retention properties.

Mixing Ratio

Mix 1 bag (40 kg) with 10 to 12 liters of clear potable water. Mix thoroughly until a uniform, lump-free paste is achieved.

Application Guide

Pre-moisten AAC blocks before laying. Apply mortar uniformly onto the block surface at a thin-bed thickness of 3 to 4 mm.

Storage & Precautions

Supplied in 40 kg moisture-proof bags. Store in a cool, dry location. Non-toxic formulation (avoid dust inhalation).
